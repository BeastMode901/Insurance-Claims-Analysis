# Insurance Claims & Risk Trends

### Project Overview
In this project, I explored an insurance database to uncover trends in claims activity and customer behavior. The analysis examined the number and value of claims, identified patterns by policy type, state, and customer demographics, and observed how claim statuses changed over time. I also determined the most frequent claim types, identified customers who filed the highest number of claims, and compared claim frequency across age groups. The goal of this project was to understand claims patterns and customer behavior, providing insights into how insurance data can support strategic business decisions.

### Analysis Questions
1. How do the number of insurance claims and their average amounts change across different months?  
2. What is the breakdown of customers by gender?  
3. Which state contains the largest customer base?  
4. Identify the customer who has submitted the most claims.  
5. How do claim amounts and frequency differ across various policy types?  
6. How do the number of claims and claim amounts vary by state?  
7. How do claim statuses (approved, pending, rejected) change over time?  
8. Which claim type occurs most frequently?  
9. How does claim frequency differ across customer age groups?  

### Summary of Findings
- Monthly claim frequency remains relatively steady, with October showing the highest average claim amount and November showing the lowest.  
- The gender distribution is nearly equal, with males and females each representing about half of the customer base.  
- California has the largest number of customers and the highest total claim amount. Georgia and several other states have the smallest customer counts. Illinois ranks second in total claims, while New York has the second-highest total claim amount.  
- Ethan Harris filed the most claims, with a total of five.  
- Claim counts are relatively similar across policy types, but Auto Insurance leads in both average and total claim amounts.  
- Claim statuses fluctuate throughout the year, with most claims being approved or pending, and fewer being rejected.  
- Storm Damage is the most frequently reported claim type.  
- Customers aged 31–40 file the most claims compared to those aged 20–30 and 40+.
